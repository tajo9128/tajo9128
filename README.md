**[BioDockify Learn](https://learn.biodockify.com)** — a free learning platform with **21 courses and 270+ AI-narrated video lessons** on:

- 🐍 **Python & Data Science** — from zero to pharma research applications
- 🤖 **AI/ML** — foundations, deep learning with PyTorch, GANs, VAEs & diffusion
- 🦙 **LLM Engineering** — fine-tuning, RAG, agents, deployment
- 💊 **Pharmacy & Drug Discovery** — pharmacology, medicinal chemistry, molecular docking

- 🏥 **Healthcare AI** — explainable AI (SHAP, GradCAM), governance, clinical judgment
